# AutoCraft Studio — commerce and measurement

## Product and pricing decisions
- Engraved wooden keychain: THB 79; 30 cm black outline wall art: THB 199 (existing slide prices).
- 30 cm full-body engraved natural MDF wall art: THB 249 (new configurable working price; distinct from outline).
- Custom design surcharge: THB 50 per distinct custom design line, once regardless of units. Show separately before submitting.
- Thailand delivery: THB 50 per order; MUIC building pickup: free, time confirmed with the team.
- Digital product previews remain labelled honestly; no neon or metal product photos.

## Custom journey
The public `/custom` page is linked prominently from the navigation and homepage. It shows three craft choices, material examples, upload guidance and an itemized estimate before cart. New custom designs and catalog purchases both enter the same account checkout and the unified admin sales workspace. Earlier inquiries appear in that workspace without being silently converted to paid orders.

Upload up to 3 reference images (4 MB total), exact car/generation, preferred product, dimensions, quantity, notes and contact. A saved request gets a 24-hour first-design target, not a guaranteed shipping date. Owner reviews feasibility and posts up to three design options in the order. Customer selects one and approves or requests a revision. Owner posts a final quote and HTTPS payment link after approval. Production follows manually verified payment. No automatic payment collection, charge, or paid conversion on link click. Pickup or shipping status and carrier tracking are shown in the account. A custom item outside standard size requires a quote; its displayed amount is an estimate until approved.

## Measurement
First-party consent-based events, persistent backend storage, first and latest tagged source, 30-minute sessions, unique browser visitors, product views, add-to-cart, checkout, server-confirmed signup, order submission and manually verified payment. Sources: IG bio, individual post, individual Reel, Story, booth QR, post QR. Every asset gets a distinct campaign/content code. Referrers alone cannot reliably distinguish IG placement; shared links remain attributed to their tag, not a guaranteed physical origin. No raw email, address, contact, upload, full URL or password in event payloads. Declined measurement, blocked scripts, browser/device changes and bots limit counts. No cross-device stitching of anonymous browsing.

## Operations and limits
Original private R2 storage remains authoritative. Customer accounts use salted password hashing and revocable HttpOnly sessions; no verified-email claim or automated password-reset email without a mail provider. Recovery codes are displayed once at signup for password reset. Admin authorization accepts the original trusted Sites owner identity or the dedicated, revocable studio password session; customer accounts never receive admin privileges. The Vercel proxy never forwards owner identity. Public Vercel forwards only explicitly permitted customer routes. Admin metrics exports exclude contact/address data. Payment confirmation is an owner action with a reference, not a gateway webhook. No Meta/GA4/Clarity account was silently created or billed.
