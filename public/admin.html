How the ordering and measurement work

Keychains ฿79 · Black outline wall art ฿199 · Full wood wall art ฿249. Custom design +฿50 per design. Thailand delivery +฿50/order or free MUIC pickup by appointment.

Custom: photo → cart → customer sign-in → checkout → studio review → design approval → final quote → payment → production. Aim for the first design/response within 24 hours. Adding a design to a cart does not submit an order.

Admin records submitted orders, private design reviews, payment verification and fulfilment. Customer notifications and payment verification are manual until external services are connected.

Campaign reports separate visits, signups, cart actions, checkout, submitted orders and verified paid orders. Measurement requires visitor consent. Instagram reach, views, saves, shares and followers come separately from Instagram Insights.

For the class report, select 42 days and export the combined summary or each-link CSV. The goals from the slides are 500 sessions, 80 valid requests, 40 paid orders / 80 units and 90% first response within one working day. The website’s response timer uses 24 clock hours.
